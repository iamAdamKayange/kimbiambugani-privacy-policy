1. Kuhusu sera hii

Sera hii inaeleza jinsi mchezo wa Kimbia Mbugani (Android, kitambulisho com.megasoftware.kimbiambugani) unavyoshughulikia taarifa. Mchezo umetengenezwa na MegaSoftware Company Limited, kampuni ya teknolojia kutoka Tanzania ("sisi").

2. Taarifa tunazokusanya

Hatukusanyi, hatuhifadhi kwenye seva zetu, wala hatushiriki taarifa zozote za kibinafsi kama jina, namba ya simu, barua pepe, mahali ulipo, picha, anwani za watu au kitambulisho cha kifaa. Mchezo hauna akaunti, matangazo, zana za takwimu (analytics) wala manunuzi ndani ya app.

3. Taarifa zinazohifadhiwa kwenye simu yako

Ili mchezo ukumbuke maendeleo yako, taarifa zifuatazo zinahifadhiwa kwenye kifaa chako tu na hazitumwi kwetu:

  • Alama bora, umbali, sarafu, tanzanite, kazi (missions) na zawadi za kila siku
  • Wahusika na mbuga ulizofungua au kuchagua
  • Mipangilio: lugha, sauti, muziki, ubora wa picha na utangulizi wa hadithi

Ukiondoa mchezo au kufuta data yake kwenye mipangilio ya simu, taarifa hizi zinafutika kabisa.

4. Ruhusa za simu

Mchezo hauombi ruhusa ya kamera, maikrofoni, mahali, anwani wala faili zako. Intaneti haihitajiki kucheza.

5. Huduma za wengine

  • Google Play: inasambaza mchezo na inaweza kukusanya taarifa kulingana na sera ya faragha ya Google.
  • Unity: mchezo umejengwa kwa injini ya Unity, ambayo inaweza kukusanya taarifa chache za kiufundi zisizokutambulisha (mf. aina ya kifaa na mfumo wake) kulingana na sera ya faragha ya Unity.

6. Viungo vya nje

Ukurasa wa "Wasanii (Credits)" una vitufe vya tovuti yetu na mitandao ya kijamii (Instagram, TikTok, X, Threads, GitHub, WhatsApp, YouTube). Ukibonyeza kimoja, kinafunguliwa kwenye kivinjari au app husika, na hapo sera ya faragha ya huduma hiyo ndiyo inayotumika. Hakuna kinachofunguliwa usipobonyeza.

7. Watoto

Kimbia Mbugani inafaa kwa rika zote. Kwa kuwa hatukusanyi taarifa za kibinafsi, hatukusanyi taarifa za watoto kwa makusudi. Mzazi akiwa na swali, awasiliane nasi hapa chini.

8. Mabadiliko ya sera hii

Tukibadilisha sera hii (mf. tukiongeza huduma mpya), tutaweka toleo jipya kwenye ukurasa huu na kubadilisha tarehe iliyo juu.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the game Kimbia Mbugani (Android, package com.megasoftware.kimbiambugani) handles information. The game is developed by MegaSoftware Company Limited, a technology company based in Tanzania ("we", "us").

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, location, photos, contacts or device identifiers. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics tools and no in-app purchases.

3. Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the following data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Best score, distance, coins, tanzanite, missions and daily rewards
  • Characters and parks you have unlocked or selected
  • Settings: language, sound, music, graphics quality and story intro

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in your phone settings permanently deletes this data.

4. Device permissions

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. No internet connection is needed to play.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Play distributes the game and may collect information under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unity: the game is built with the Unity engine, which may collect limited, non-identifying technical information (for example device model and operating system) under the Unity Privacy Policy.

6. External links

The in-game "Credits" page contains buttons for our website and social media (Instagram, TikTok, X, Threads, GitHub, WhatsApp, YouTube). Tapping one opens it in your browser or the relevant app, where that service's own privacy policy applies. Nothing opens unless you tap it.

7. Children

Kimbia Mbugani is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children. Parents with questions can contact us below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example, if we add new services), we will publish the updated version on this page and update the date above.
